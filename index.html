03 / ABOUT THE ATLAS

A new symmetry.
An unfolding story.

Carroll–Weyl gauged null strings explore how local worldsheet rescalings shape the geometry, constraints, and quantization of strings at zero tension. This collection follows that developing literature, including different interpretations of the symmetry and its target-space meaning.

Scope & inclusion criteria +

The collection starts with arXiv:2605.12414 and follows papers directly concerned with Carroll–Weyl symmetry and null strings in arXiv’s hep-th category. Searches include spelling variants, follow-up papers, and relevant references.

Titles, authors, categories, and original submission dates are checked against arXiv records. Summaries describe each paper’s claims; inclusion does not imply agreement between the papers. This is a reviewed bibliography, with the review date shown above, rather than a guarantee of exhaustive coverage.
