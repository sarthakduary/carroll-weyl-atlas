# Sources and scope

Reviewed 13 November 2026.

This collection contains 18 arXiv hep-th papers directly concerned with Carroll–Weyl gauged null strings. It begins with arXiv:2605.12414 and includes direct responses, geometric reinterpretations, quantization studies, open strings, and holographic applications. Different interpretations are represented without implying agreement.

## Verification and coverage

Titles, authors, original submission dates, and primary categories were checked against current arXiv records. Versions are consolidated into one entry. Discovery used spelling variants (Carroll–Weyl, Carroll-Weyl, null strings), references in the follow-up literature, and the INSPIRE citation graphs of records 3154828 and 3159259. This is a reviewed bibliography, not a guarantee of exhaustive coverage.

The user-supplied INSPIRE record 3159259 is the first *Gauged and Reloaded* paper (2605.25817). The earlier starting paper (2605.12414) corresponds to INSPIRE record 3154828.

Summaries are short editorial paraphrases of each paper’s scope and claims. Quantum statements are qualified by representation or quantization scheme. Current titles are retained; the spelling “Backgorund” in 2611.09420 follows the official arXiv title.

## Scope exclusions

A citation to the Carroll–Weyl literature alone is insufficient for inclusion. The following adjacent papers were checked but excluded from the direct collection:

- [2604.01883](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01883) — Optional background only; do not count as a direct Carroll–Weyl gauged null-string paper.
- [2609.36026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36026) — Independent Carroll–Weyl boundary scalings in flat holography, rather than gauged null strings; omit from the core bibliography.
- [2609.08874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08874) — Carroll non-BPS D-branes with restricted χ symmetry, rather than the gauged null-string theory; omit from the core bibliography.
- [2609.00797](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00797) — Dp-branes with restricted symmetry and worldvolume gauge fields; omit from direct null-string coverage.
- [2609.07845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07845) — The paper studies ILST flipped-vacuum scattering. Footnote 1 explicitly says it does not consider the additional gauged symmetries, so it is outside the direct Carroll–Weyl gauged bibliography.
- [2610.41582](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41582) — General non-Lorentzian superstring construction. Abstract and full HTML do not directly study Carroll-Weyl gauging; citation alone is insufficient for core inclusion.
